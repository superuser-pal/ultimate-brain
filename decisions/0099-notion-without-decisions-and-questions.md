# 0099 — Notion without the Decisions and Questions databases: decisions in the project page, questions and risks you follow up as tasks

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-16
**Release:** 0.16.0
**Amends:** [0071](0071-a-simpler-notion.md) §3 and §4 (decisions and questions no longer get a Notion page; a risk stays a question note, followed up as a task when you say so); [0048](0048-modules-and-simpler-rows.md) and [0078](0078-onboarding-and-early-access-in-0-14-0.md) §2 (the Knowledge module is retired); [0049](0049-documents.md) (Promote to document is asked in the session, without the button); [0055](0055-domains-capture-and-review.md) and [0018](0018-notion-tasks-mapping.md) (`Track: Waiting On —` also tracks a question or a risk, and Tasks gains a Kind); [0096](0096-one-place-to-decide.md) (0.16.0 also removes these two databases)
**Keeps:** decisions and questions as notes in Git, extracted at every ingest and used by every answer and report (0005, 0034); supersession, assessment and lifecycle (0005); the project's Brief and Decision log and the area's Brief (0041); 0071 §4's rule that a to-do is never a question; Documents as the home of what you keep (0049, 0071); 0.15.0 as the feature cutoff
**Amended by:** [0100](0100-keep-a-task-as-a-document.md) (2026-11-16; 0.17.0): Tasks also gains Document asked and the Keep as document button (§4)

## Context

On 2026-11-16 the author reviewed the two databases of the Knowledge module, to make the Notion layer simpler to understand before beta testers duplicate the template. After 0.16.0 removes Review (0096), Brain Data still holds thirteen databases.

- **Decisions** matter as history in context: why a project went one way. That history is already on every project page, in the Brief toggle (the five most recent) and the Decision log toggle (all of them, current and superseded), and in reports ("Decisions in force"). A row per decision in its own database adds a place to look, not a thing to do.
- **Questions** mixed two things. 0071 found many were to-dos, deleted by hand. The open questions that matter are either followed up by someone, which is what a task is for, or wait for a later meeting or document, which the brain notices on its own. Risks shared the database with Kind Risk.
- The author asked whether questions and risks belong in the Inbox, or in one general database with bookmarks, URLs and AI artifacts. The Inbox is the history of what came in; a question is what the brain concluded from an input, so the Inbox would mix outputs with inputs and its Brain Status would not apply. A general database with a Kind column trades one database for one harder to understand. Documents already holds what you keep: whole and partial inputs, learned explanations, reports, promoted notes and the artifacts of a connected workflow.

The author's grill, Q1 to Q9, chose the recommended option each time. Q3 was not answered and the record takes its recommended option; the author can change it on the PR.

## Decision

1. **The Decisions and Questions databases are removed** (Q1, Q2). Decisions and questions stay notes in Git, extracted at every ingest as today, and nothing changes in how the brain answers from them. Notion shows them on the pages that need them: a project's **Brief** and **Decision log** toggles, an area's **Brief**, and reports. Brain Data keeps eleven databases: Inbox, Meetings, Projects, Areas, People, Domains, Orgs, Documents, Tasks, Goals, Key Results. The Knowledge module is retired: decisions and questions are always extracted, and never published as rows.
2. **A decision with no project and no area** is reached on request: an Ask and its report, or the agent session (Q3, the record's reading).
3. **What you follow up is a task** (Q6). A question you must find out yourself is a task To Do; one you asked someone is a task Waiting On; a risk you want to act on is a task. The question note stays in Git, linked to its task. Questions and risks you don't follow up show only in the Brief.
4. **Tasks are filtered by question and risk** (the author's requirement after Q6). Tasks gains a **Kind** property (select: Task, Question, Risk). A task the brain creates from a question has Kind Question, from a risk Kind Risk; any other task has Kind Task or no Kind. The Tasks page gains two tabs, **Questions** and **Risks** (Kind is Question or Risk, not Done), across every project. On a mapped Tasks database (0018), the connect step offers to add Kind; without it, such a task's title starts with `Question: ` or `Risk: `, so a title filter still finds them.
5. **A question becomes a task only when you say so** (Q7): "track it" in the agent session, or the token `Track: Waiting On —` on a question or risk line in the minutes' "For the brain" toggle, the token that already tracks someone else's commitment (0055). The brain never adds the token and never creates a task from a question on its own. With the Tasks module off, nothing is tracked, and the session says so.
6. **An answer closes it in the session.** When a later input answers a tracked question, the session shows the answer and its task and asks; on your yes the answer is recorded (0096 §3) and the brain sets the task Done once, as it set a question Answered before. A task you mark Done in Notion leaves the question open until it is answered or you drop it in the session.
7. **The Brief's section is "Open questions and risks"** (Q8): one list, each risk labelled `(risk)`, in the project's Brief and the area's Brief. A tracked question's line links its task.
8. **Existing spaces** (Q9). The 0.16.0 Notion migration, the one that archives Review, first reads back any Status you set on a question in Notion, then archives both databases (their pages kept in Archive, nothing deleted), removes their views, relations and toggles, and adds Kind and the two tabs to Tasks. The one-off tidy (capture-and-review §6.2) then offers each open question as a task, one yes at a time.
9. **What leaves Notion with them.** The Knowledge page keeps its other tabs (Documents, People, Orgs, Inbox) and loses Decisions and Questions; project and area pages lose "What have we decided here?" and "What's still open here?"; meeting pages lose their Decisions and Questions toggles (the minutes still list both); the Inbox loses its Decisions column; Promote to document becomes a request in the session ("promote the decision on … to a document"), and the promoted Document keeps its `promoted_from` in Git; a connected workflow's decision has no page, its artifact's Document is unchanged. Brain Assistant's Prep, Write up and Refresh report read the project page's Brief and Decision log and the Tasks Kind instead of the two databases.
10. **Bookmarks, URLs and AI artifacts get no new database** (Q4). A URL that comes in is an Inbox capture; what you keep is a Document. Bookmarks and session artifacts as features of their own are post-1.0 ideas (docs/v2-ideas.md).
11. **In 0.16.0, before the invites** (Q5), in the same migration, skills release and template as 0096. It removes and simplifies; Kind on Tasks replaces the Kind of the removed Questions database, so 0.15.0 stays the feature cutoff.

## Consequences

- Contracts first (CC-313): `spec/schema.md` (no Notion page or mapping for `decision` and `question`, the Brief's section, a question's `task` without `dropped`, the Knowledge module and its `brain.json` keys retired), `spec/capture-and-review.md` §6 (the token on a question or risk, "track it", the tidy), `spec/cli.md` (publish, `sync tasks` with Kind, `brain upgrade`'s step for `brain.json`), `spec/assistant.md`, and `docs/notion-space-design.md`. Then the CLI (CC-314), the skills (CC-298), the Notion migration and Brain Assistant (CC-299), and the template (U-33).
- The template's Example rows for Decisions and Questions go; the example question becomes a Tasks Example row with Kind Question and Status Waiting On, in the same story (0087's "an example in every database" holds for the eleven).
- `notion` page ids on decision and question notes stop being written; the contract says whether `brain upgrade` clears them.
- Brain Assistant can no longer list decisions as rows: "what did we decide about X" in Notion reads the project's Decision log, and anything wider is a report or the session.
- A user without the Tasks module has no cross-project list of open questions: each project's Brief shows its own.
