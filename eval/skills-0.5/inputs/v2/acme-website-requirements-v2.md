# Acme website: requirements (v2)

Prepared by Maria López for the Acme website project, 12 December 2026.

## Background

Acme wants a new marketing website to replace the 2019 site. The agency team met Acme's marketing group in November and agreed the scope below. Maria López is the product manager on Acme's side.

## Requirements

1. Login with email and password for partners.
2. Blog with categories and search.
3. Pages load in under 2 seconds on a 4G connection.
4. Content migration from the old site finished by 15 March 2027.
5. Hosting in the EU only.

## Budget

The approved budget is 80000 EUR, including the first year of hosting.
