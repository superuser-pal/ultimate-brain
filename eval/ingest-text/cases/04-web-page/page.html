Acme launch update: two more languages

Published 6 December 2026 · Acme newsroom

The new Acme website will launch in English, German and Spanish. German and Spanish were planned for a later phase; customer research moved them into the launch scope.

"Two thirds of our enquiries come from Germany and Spain, so launching in English only made no sense," said Jonas Weber, Head of Marketing at Acme.

What this means

  • All product pages and the blog are translated before launch.
  • Translation is done by an agency; Acme's marketing team reviews every page.
