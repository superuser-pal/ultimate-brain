# Harbour pilot: station survey

Harbourline Bikes · Operations · 18 November 2026 · by Dario Meck

## Stations

The harbour district has 14 candidate stations with 160 e-bikes between them. We walked all 14 on 16 and 17 November.

Twelve stations are ready: power, docking rails and signage are in place. Two are not: Pier 3 and Ferry Terminal have no power connection for the chargers yet. The utility quotes eight to ten weeks for a new connection.

## Expected use

From the current app's data, we expect 45 rides per station per weekday and 70 per station per weekend day during the pilot.

## Decision

On 17 November 2026 Ines Varga and Dario Meck decided that the pilot starts with 12 stations; Pier 3 and Ferry Terminal join once their power connections are in.
