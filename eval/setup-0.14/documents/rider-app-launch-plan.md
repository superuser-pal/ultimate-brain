# Rider App 2.0: launch plan (v3)

Harbourline Bikes · Product · 20 November 2026 · owner: Ines Varga

## Where we are

The new rider app is feature-complete. The public launch is planned for 10 February 2027, followed by a six-week pricing pilot in the harbour district. Checkout must be certified by Paylane before launch.

## Who does what before launch

- Tomas Brandt submits the final build to Paylane for certification by 24 November 2026.
- Priya Okafor delivers the final screen designs for onboarding by 5 December 2026.
- Dario Meck confirms the delivery date of the new harbour docking stations by 1 December 2026.
- I will finalise the beta tester list (40 riders from the current app) by 28 November 2026.
- I will sign off the app store listing text by 9 January 2027.

## Risks

- Paylane certification takes four to six weeks from the final build. If it slips, the launch date slips.
- The harbour docking stations may arrive after launch.

## Later
