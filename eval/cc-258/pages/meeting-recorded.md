<transcript>
Ines Varga: Morning everyone. Thanks for joining the Rider App 2.0 sync. Tomas, Priya and Dario are here, so we have everyone we need. I want to cover the launch date, the onboarding redesign and the pricing pilot, in that order.

Tomas Brandt: Before we start, the coffee machine on the third floor is broken again, so bring your own.

Ines Varga: Noted. Launch date first. Tomas, where are we with the payment provider?

Tomas Brandt: The payment provider still hasn't finished certifying our checkout flow. They told us last week that certification takes four to six weeks from the day they receive the final build. We sent the final build on the twenty-fourth of November, so the earliest realistic date is early January for certification alone.

Ines Varga: So the tenth of February launch is not safe anymore.

Tomas Brandt: Not with a proper beta in between. I would not put riders on a checkout that has been live for less than three weeks.

Dario Meck: From the operations side a March launch is actually better. The new docking stations at the harbour arrive in late February, and I would rather launch with them than add them two weeks later.

Ines Varga: Then let's agree on it. We move the public launch from the tenth of February to the third of March 2027. Everyone fine with that?

Priya Okafor: Fine for design.

Tomas Brandt: Fine, as long as certification does not slip again. That is my worry, honestly. If they find one more issue in the refund flow, we lose another two weeks and March is gone too.

Ines Varga: Tomas, can you get a firm certification date from them?

Tomas Brandt: I will get a written certification date from the provider by Friday the fifth of December.

Ines Varga: Next, onboarding. Priya, over to you.

Priya Okafor: We tested the new onboarding with eight riders at Fernhill Studio last Tuesday. It is three screens now instead of seven: the welcome screen, the map permission and the payment card. Six of the eight riders finished it in under ninety seconds.

Dario Meck: What happened to the phone verification step?

Priya Okafor: That is the question I wanted to bring. Phone verification is where four of the eight riders stopped and asked why we need it. My proposal is to drop phone verification at signup and only ask for it before the first e-bike ride.

Ines Varga: I like that. Tomas, any technical reason to keep it at signup?

Tomas Brandt: No. The fraud checks run at the first unlock anyway, not at signup.

Ines Varga: Then we drop phone verification from signup and ask for it before the first ride.

Dario Meck: One thing we don't know yet: whether the city requires an ID check for e-bikes. The new municipal rules mention identity checks for motorised vehicles and nobody has read whether that covers pedal-assist bikes.

Ines Varga: That one we can't settle today. Dario, you have the regulation PDF, don't you? Forward it to me and I'll pass it to Tomas. Actually, I'll just forward the city regulation PDF to Tomas myself, I have it in my inbox.

Priya Okafor: And the copy on the three screens is still placeholder text. I need final wording before we build the screens.

Ines Varga: That's mine. I will write the onboarding copy for all three screens by the twelfth of December, and I'll keep it under forty words a screen.

Priya Okafor: Perfect. Can you also send me the brand font files? The ones in the shared drive are the old version.

Ines Varga: Sure, I'll send you the brand font files today.

Ines Varga: Last subject, the pricing pilot.

Dario Meck: The pilot runs in the harbour district for six weeks after launch. My suggestion is that we keep the per-minute price at twenty-five cents for the pilot, the same as the current app, so we only test the new app and not a new price.

Ines Varga: Agreed. We keep twenty-five cents per minute during the pilot.

Priya Okafor: We could also try a weekend pass, something like nine euros for unlimited rides on Saturday and Sunday. Just an idea, I haven't thought it through.

Ines Varga: Interesting, but not for the pilot. Let's keep it in mind.

Dario Meck: For the board, they will want to know how many stations the pilot covers. I will send the station count and the expected rides per station by the tenth of December.

Ines Varga: Thanks. Then I will draft the pilot pricing brief for the board by the seventeenth of December, using Dario's numbers.

Tomas Brandt: Are we sure the board meeting is the eighteenth?

Ines Varga: It's the eighteenth, yes. One more thing for me: I need to book a thirty-minute call with the payment provider for next week, so we hear the certification plan from them directly.

Ines Varga: That's everything. Thanks all, and enjoy the team lunch on Thursday.
